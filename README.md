# VEDA Technology – Task 18: Region Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes regional sales and profitability using the Superstore dataset as part of VEDA Technology's Data Analytics Internship.

The objective is to compare business performance across four regions, identify top-performing and underperforming regions, and communicate findings through Excel and Power BI dashboards.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Data Visualization

## Analysis Performed
- Aggregated total sales by region.
- Aggregated total profit by region.
- Ranked regions based on sales and profit.
- Compared regional performance using bar charts.
- Developed separate Excel and Power BI dashboards.
- Identified opportunities for business improvement.

## Key Findings

| Region | Total Sales | Total Profit | Sales Rank | Profit Rank |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Central | $2,548,575.92 | $520,465.07 | 1 | 1 |
| East | $2,433,698.55 | $379,190.71 | 2 | 2 |
| West | $2,395,112.35 | $307,642.20 | 3 | 3 |
| South | $1,598,275.04 | $104,887.73 | 4 | 4 |

## Business Insights
- Central emerged as the strongest region in both sales and profitability.
- South recorded the lowest sales and profit.
- Sales and profit rankings were consistent across all four regions.
- Regional performance comparison highlights where management should investigate opportunities to improve profitability.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate the factors contributing to South's lower profitability.
- Examine Central's performance to identify successful business patterns.
- Monitor both sales and profit to support better regional decisions.

## Project Deliverables
- Excel regional performance dashboard.
- Power BI interactive regional performance dashboard.
- Regional sales and profit comparisons.
- Ranking and business insights.
